Savora — Privacy Policy

Applies to the app Savora for Android and iOS (package and bundle ID com.ardentdigitals.savora), published by Ardent Digitals.
Effective date: 1 September 2026. Last updated: 8 October 2026 (iOS added).

This policy explains what Savora does with information. It is short because the app does very little with your data, and nothing you enter ever leaves your phone.

Savora has no accounts

There is no sign-up, no login and no password. You are never asked to identify yourself, and no profile is created for you on any server.

What we store on your device

If you use the profile screen, the following is stored on your phone only, in the operating system's standard private app storage (Android or iOS):

  • the name and email address you type in, if any;
  • your chosen default district;
  • which offers you bookmarked;
  • your notification preferences.

None of this is transmitted to Ardent Digitals or to anyone else. It is not copied to any server, and we have no way to see it.

Location

Savora asks for location permission only when you tap “Use current location” on the profile screen. The app then reads your position once and converts it into a district name — for example “Dhanmondi, Dhaka” — using the geocoding service built into your device (Android's or Apple's geocoder). Only that district name is saved, on your phone. Your coordinates are not stored by the app and are never sent to us.

Location is processed only for as long as it takes to work out the district name, and is not retained afterwards. You never have to grant this permission: you can pick your district from a list instead, and the rest of the app works the same.

What the app downloads

To show offers, Savora reads a public, read-only catalogue of discounts over an encrypted HTTPS connection. These requests carry no name, no email address, no location and no advertising identifier.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose. Savora contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no tracking SDK.

One service provider is involved in running the app: Supabase, which hosts the offer catalogue the app downloads. Like any web host, Supabase processes the network-level information needed to deliver a response, such as your IP address. It receives no name, no email address and no location from Savora.

How we protect data

All network traffic between the app and its hosting provider uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Anything you enter stays in the private per-app storage of Android or iOS, which the operating system keeps isolated from other apps on your device. Because none of your personal data is transmitted to us, there is no copy of it on our side that could be exposed.

Data retention and deletion

We retain no personal data, because we never receive any. Everything Savora stores lives on your own device for as long as you keep the app installed.

To delete it, do either of the following:

  • uninstall Savora — this removes all of it; or
  • on Android, go to Settings → Apps → Savora → Storage → Clear data, which resets the app to its first-run state. On iOS, uninstalling the app is the way to remove its data.

No request to us is needed, and there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you would like confirmation of this in writing, contact us at the address below.

What Savora does not do

  • It does not share, route, resell or otherwise make use of your internet connection or bandwidth for any purpose other than fetching the offers you see in the app.
  • It runs nothing in the background. On iOS it uses no background modes. On Android it does not hold the FOREGROUND_SERVICE permission, so it cannot keep a service running in the background, and it declares no boot receiver, so it never starts itself when your device boots. Closing the app stops everything it was doing.
  • It shows no advertising and reads no advertising identifier.
  • It contains no analytics or tracking code.
  • It does not read your contacts, photos, files, messages, call logs or installed app list.

Permissions the app requests

On iOS the only permission is location “While Using the App”, for “Use current location”, and only if you grant it. On Android:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to download offers.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — only for “Use current location”, only while you are using the app, and only if you grant them.

The app requests no other permissions. In particular it does not request FOREGROUND_SERVICE, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED or SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW.

For completeness: the third-party location plugin Savora uses (geolocator) declares a service in the app manifest for apps that follow a user's location continuously. Savora does not use that feature — it reads your position once, only when you tap “Use current location” — and without the FOREGROUND_SERVICE permission that service cannot run in the background.

Children

Savora is intended for a general audience aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
